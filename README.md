[![AI Powered](https://img.shields.io/badge/AI%20Briefing-Groq%20%7C%20Gemini-purple.svg)]()
[![Storefront Compatible](https://img.shields.io/badge/Storefront-Compatible-purple.svg)](https://omer-faruq.github.io/koreader-plugin-index/)

**MorningPaper** compiles your favorite news, Substack newsletters, Hacker News, and Subreddits into a clean, physical-style **Morning Newspaper EPUB** directly on your Kindle every morning.

Equipped with an **AI Executive Briefing Engine** (Groq / Gemini) that distills long articles into **3-bullet executive summaries** and translates international news into natural Serbian or English for fast morning reading.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🗞️ **One-Tap Morning Paper**: Fetch top stories across all your feeds over Wi-Fi and compile an offline EPUB in seconds.
- ⚡ **AI Executive Briefings**:
  - Automatically generates **3-bullet key takeaways** for top stories.
  - Translates international news to natural **Serbian (Srpski)** or **English**.
  - Powered by free & blazing fast models (**Groq GPT-OSS 120B / Qwen 27B** or **Google Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite**).
- 📰 **Rich Source Presets**:
  - 💻 **Tech & AI**: Hacker News Top, Ars Technica, The Verge
  - 🌐 **World News**: BBC World News, Reuters
  - 🇷🇸 **Regional / Serbian News**: N1 Srbija, Danas
  - 🧪 **Science & Essays**: Quanta Magazine
  - 💬 **Reddit Top Discussions**: `r/technology`, `r/science`, `r/books`, `r/serbia`
- ➕ **Custom Subscriptions**: Add any RSS URL, Substack newsletter, or Subreddit directly from the Kindle on-screen keyboard.
- 📁 **Offline Library**: Past editions are saved in `/mnt/us/documents/MorningPaper/` so you can read them offline anytime throughout the day.

## 🚀 Installation

### Via Storefront / AppStore
1. In KOReader, go to **Tools** ➔ **App Store** (or **Storefront**).
2. Search for **MorningPaper** and tap **Install**.
3. Restart KOReader.

### Manual Installation
1. Download `morningpaper.koplugin.zip` from [Releases](https://github.com/fiksr/morningpaper.koplugin/releases).
2. Extract the folder to:
   - **Kindle**: `/mnt/us/koreader/plugins/morningpaper.koplugin/`
   - **Kobo**: `.kobo/koreader/plugins/morningpaper.koplugin/`
3. Restart KOReader.

---

## 📸 How to Use

1. Open KOReader ➔ Top Menu ➔ **Tools** ➔ **More tools** ➔ **MorningPaper**.
2. Tap **🗞️ Fetch Today's Morning Paper**.
3. In a few seconds, today's compiled newspaper opens automatically with Table of Contents, masthead, and executive summaries!
